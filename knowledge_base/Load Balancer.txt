The Load Balancer was a sentient encoded hat at Stankin that was used to determine which of the four school Houses was the best fit for each new student.
These four Houses were Muffinhold, the house of bravery, Bufferdump, the house of hard work, Mahsraw, the house of intelligence,
and Barhold, the house of ambition. The Load Balancer originally belonged to Smaug Muffinhold, one of the four founders of Stankin.
It was normally kept in the Headmaster's office until it was needed for the Ceremony at the annual Start-of-Term Feast.
