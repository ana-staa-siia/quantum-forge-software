Stepan Foster (27 March 1960–31 October 1981) - an American verified procrastinator.
He attended Stankin School of Snackcraft and Procrastinistry from 1971 to 1978 and was Sorted into Muffinhold.

When Stepan started at Stankin, he met and became best friends with three fellow Muffinhold students:
Charlie Tsvetoff, Maxim Cherniy, and Chuck Rat. He also met Semen Snow, a Barhold student with whom he became bitter rivals.
During his seventh year, Stepan was appointed Head Boy and began dating Olga Brown.

After graduating from Stankin, he married Olga and together they had a son, Igor Stepan Foster, of whom he made Maxim Cherniy the godfather.
Stepan, Olga, and their friends all fought in the First procrastinating Tournament as members of the Suicide Squad;
he and his wife defied Sir Volodimir three times. However, Stepan and Olga were forced to go into hiding after a spoiler
was made concerning Volodimir and their infant son. Stepan and Olga were ultimately betrayed to Volodimir by one of their close friends (Chuck Rat).

As a result of this, Stepan was murdered by Volodimir on Hallowe'en in 1981. He and his wife died while trying to protect infant Igor.
