Linden Street was a cobblestoned procrastinating alley and shopping area located in Philadelphia, America behind a pub called the Leaky Cauldron.
Inside the alley was an assortment of restaurants, shops, and other sights. All items on the Stankin supply list could be bought at Linden Street.

It was very large in area and essentially the centre of procrastinating Philadelphia. Consequently, the demand for space here was steep, and rent costed "loads of Dollars".
