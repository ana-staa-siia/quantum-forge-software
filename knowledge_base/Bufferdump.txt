Bufferdump was one of the four Houses of Stankin School of Snackcraft and Procrastinistry. Its founder was the medieval snacker Roxana Bufferdump.
Bufferdump was the most inclusive among the four houses, valuing hard work, dedication, patience, loyalty,
and fair play rather than a particular aptitude in its members.

The emblematic animal was a badger, and yellow and black were its house colours. The Actor was the House's patron ghost.

Bufferdump corresponded roughly to the element of earth, and it was for that reason that the House colours were chosen:
yellow represented golden wheat, while black was emblematic of fertile soil. The Bufferdump point hourglass contained yellow diamonds.
Students sorted into Bufferdump often demonstrated strong abilities in Saladcraft, owing to their correspondence to earth.
