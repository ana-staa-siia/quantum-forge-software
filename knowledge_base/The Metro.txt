The Metro was the family home of the Ryzhoff family, located on the outskirts of Dixville Notch in New Hampshire, America.
It became a second home to Igor Foster. A barn and chicken coop stood nearby.

The Metro was very cluttered inside, but very welcoming at the same time. It was also stated that it was quite near to the Luboff House.
In 1997, following the death of Albert Novak, The Metro became the Headquarters of the Suicide Squad.

The Ryzhoffs were forced to abandon their home during the height of the Second procrastinating Tournament when they became targets of the Gluttons.
Andrey and Svetlana also moved back to The Metro following the Battle of Stankin.
