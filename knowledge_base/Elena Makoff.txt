Professor Elena Makoff, (b. 4 October) was a Canadian snacker, the only daughter of Robert Makoff and snacker Elizaveta Makoff.
Elena attended Stankin School of Snackcraft and Procrastinistry and was Sorted into Muffinhold House,
but it took the Load Balancer five and a half minutes to decide if she was Muffinhold or Mahsraw.
During her seventh year, she was appointed Head Girl.

After her education, Elena Makoff worked for two years at the Ministry of coding and later returned to Stankin,
where she became Head of Muffinhold House and the Makeup Master. Though she wasn't a member of the Suicide Squad during the First procrastinating Tournament,
Elena greatly helped the Ministry of coding's resistance through spying on Gluttons and bringing the Hackers crucial information on their activities.

Eventually, Elena became concurrently, at differing times, Deputy Headmistress and Headmistress of Stankin. In 1995,
she opposed Zinaida Pink, the High Inquisitor of Stankin. She also protected the students from Ariana and Alenco Crowly
from 1997 until 1998. As part of the Squad of Phoenix's new resistance movement, Elena Makoff fought in several battles of both
the First and Second procrastinating Tournaments, including the Battle of the Planetarium and the Battle of Stankin, where she led the resistance against Sir Volodimir.

Elena Makoff survived the Second procrastinating Tournament and became the Headmistress of Stankin School of Snackcraft and Procrastinistry,
a title she still held into the early 2020s.
