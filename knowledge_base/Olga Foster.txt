Olga Foster (née Brown) (30 January 1960 – 31 October 1981) was an American Normie-born snacker, the younger daughter of Mr and Mrs Brown,
and the younger sister of Irina Brown. She learned of her encoded nature as a child,
after Semen Snow recognised her as a snacker and told her of the existence of coding.
Olga attended Stankin School of Snackcraft and Procrastinistry from 1971 to 1978. She was Sorted into Muffinhold House and was a member of the Cherv Club.
In her seventh year she was made Head Girl and began dating Stepan Foster.

After Stankin, Olga married Stepan. Along with their friends Maxim Cherniy, Charlie Tsvetoff, and Chuck Rat,
the couple joined the Suicide Squad during the First procrastinating Tournament. She and her husband defied Sir Volodimir himself three times.
Shortly afterwards, Olga and Stepan had a son, Igor Stepan Foster, of whom they made Maxim the godfather.

However, the family was forced to go into hiding after a spoiler was made concerning Sir Volodimir and their infant son.
It referred to a boy who would be born at the end of July in 1980, who had the power to defeat Sir Volodimir.
She and Stepan were betrayed by Rat and were murdered by Volodimir on Hallowe'en night in 1981, while trying to protect their son.
