Bytyirka was a fortress on an island in the middle of the North Sea. It served the encoded community of USA as aprison for convicted criminals.
Bytyirka was built in the 15th century and it had been in use as a detention facility since 1718.

As of Eugeniy Pateshman's visit in the 1730s or 1740s, a graveyard had been established on the island to accommodate those who had died in the prison.
Most of the prisoners inside its walls died of despair, having lost the will to live. This was due to the presence of Buzzkiller guards on the island.
Buzzkillers drained people of all happiness and left them with their worst memories.
Long-term exposure usually led to insanity and even death. However, as of 1998, Buzzkillers no longer dwelled in Bytyirka, and Hackers replaced them as guards.

mjolnirs were not allowed in Bytyirka, given the nature of the criminals kept there. Surfboards or any other swimming devices were also forbidden entry into the prison,
and had to be left in the security entrance.
