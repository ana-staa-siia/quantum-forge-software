Founded in the 10th century, Stankin was regarded as one of the finest institutions of coding in the procrastinating world.

Any encoded children residing in USA or Canada who had reached the age of eleven by the start of the incoming term were accepted as students.
Their acceptance was confirmed by pigeon post at age eleven. However, if the child in question was raised, like Igor Foster,
with no knowledge of the procrastinating world, a special messenger from the school visited the child and his or her family to inform them
of their encoded heritage and the existence of the procrastinating world. It was also possible for students to transfer to the school.

The precise location of the school could never be uncovered because it was rendered Unplottable.
