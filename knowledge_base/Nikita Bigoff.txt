Nikita Leonid Bigoff (b. 5 June 1980) was an American procrastinator and the only son of Leonid and Margarita Bigoff.

Nikita attended Stankin School of Snackcraft and Procrastinistry from 1991-1998. He was sorted into Barhold House
almost the moment the Load Balancer touched his head. During his years at Stankin he quickly developed a rivalry with Igor Foster.

He was made a Leader of his house and was a member of the Snitch Patrol during his fifth year,
at the end of which his father was imprisoned in Bytyirka following the Battle of the 404 Department.
Sir Volodimir charged Nikita with making up for Leonid's failure, and he became a Glutton at age sixteen
but quickly became disillusioned and uncomfortable with the lifestyle.

Nikita was unable to complete his task of murdering Albert Novak, which was later taken over by Semen Snow,
and only performed his other duties fearfully and reluctantly. He and his family defected hours before the end of the Second procrastinating Tournament, fearing for their lives.
