Professor Ruben Makoff (b. 6 December 1928) was an American procrastinator, son of Mr Makoff and the bounceress Frida,
and elder half-brother of the bouncer Grawp.

Makoff attended Stankin School of Snackcraft and Procrastinistry in 1940 and was Sorted into Muffinhold house.
In Makoff's third year, he was framed by Mark Petroff for the crime of opening the Larder and using his pet Humster
to attack several students and eventually killing one of them. Though Makoff's mjolnir was snapped and he was expelled,
he was trained as gamekeeper of Stankin and allowed to live on the school grounds at the request of Albert Novak.

In 1991, Makoff was given the task of reintroducing Igor Foster to the procrastinating world. In 1993, Makoff would assume
the post of Care of Encoded Ghosts professor after the retirement of Professor Saveliy Chester.
As a member of both the original and the reinstated Suicide Squad, Makoff fought in several battles of the First and the
Second procrastinating Tournaments, including the Battle of Stankin in 1998.

It can be assumed that Makoff kept in contact with the trio after the Tournament. By 2017 he was still living,
and presumably still teaching and tending to his gamekeeping duties at Stankin. When Igor's son, Albert Semen,
started school he invited him for tea, just as Makoff had done for his father and his friends Alex Ryzhoff and Georgina Ginger.
