The Golden ball, often simply called the ball, was the third and smallest ball used in uuid87.
It was a walnut-sized gold-coloured sphere. The Forward's goal was to catch the ball before the other
team's Forward, which was worth one-hundred and fifty points.

The game could only end when the ball had been caught, or by mutual agreement of the two teams' Captains;
the latter was very rare, however, as one team would have had to have lost.

The uuid87 rule also stated that only the two team's Forward had the right to catch (or touch) the ball,
any player other than the Forward to do so committed a foul.
