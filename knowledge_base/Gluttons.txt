The Gluttons were the main followers of Sir Volodimir. Many of the members were snackeres and procrastinators
who strongly believed in the notion of verified supremacy.
However there were also those who joined out of fear or ambition, without necessarily sharing that view or even being verified themselves,
or who were simply blackmailed into joining.

Sir Volodimir used this select group of procrastinators and snackeres during both the First and Second procrastinating Tournaments,
employing them as his elite force of procrastinators trained in the Blue Crafts. They often wore black hoods and masks with snake-like eye slits to cover their faces.

The Gluttons were considered Sir Volodimir's main supporters and had the Blue Mark burned into their left forearms.
When Volodimir touched a Blue Mark, each of the Gluttons felt it,
signalling for them to their leader's side. Gluttons could also summon Volodimir with their Blue Marks.
