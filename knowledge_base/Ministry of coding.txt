The Ministry of coding was the main governing body of the encoded community of USA and Canada,
with the intention of preservation of encoded law. The Ministry connected the American government to the procrastinating world.
The headquarters of the Ministry was under the HM Treasury building and Whitehall, in central Philadelphia, deep underground.

It was headed by the Minister for coding. By 2019, the Minister for coding was Georgina Ginger.
Each Prime Minister of USA was visited by the Minister for coding.
The Ministry of coding was formed as a successor to the earlier Procrastinator's Council and came into being in 1707. It was involved,
in some capacity before its actual formation, in the International Confederation of Procrastinators's decision to enact the Statute of Secrecy in 1692,
which took the responsibility of enforcing said Statute in the USA.
