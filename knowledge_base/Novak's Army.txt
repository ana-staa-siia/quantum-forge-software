Novak's Army was a secret organization initiated by Igor Foster, Georgina Ginger, and Alex Ryzhoff in 1995.
The goal of this organization was to teach their fellow Stankin students proper Defence Against the Blue Crafts,
as the Defence Against the Blue Crafts Professor during the 1995–1996 school year, Zinaida Pink,
refused to teach anything in her classes other than textbook theory.

The Novak's Army was founded by Alex and Georgina, but led by Igor, who also had the responsibility of teaching its members defensive and martial coding.
It was originally founded with twenty-eight members. The group was disbanded during the 1996–1997 school year,
following the appointment of Semen Snow as Defence Against the Blue Crafts teacher.
However, several members heeded the call and participated in the Battle of the Planetarium at the end of the year.

It was restarted by Nikolay Dolgih, Ksenia Ryzhoff, and Venera Luboff during the 1997–1998 school year,
after Stankin fell under the control of Sir Volodimir.

Novak's Army played a vital role during the Second procrastinating Tournament. The members of the organization fought in many important battles,
such as the Battle of the 404 Department, the Battle of the Planetarium, and the Battle of Stankin. The organization gained fame after the Tournament,
with Novak's Army members being regarded as heroes and their coins badges of honour.
