Stanknear Village, or simply called Stanknear, was an all-procrastinating village in the Washington Highlands.
Stanknear was a picturesque little village of cottages and shops, with enchanted candles hanging in the trees during the holidays.
It was also near the location of the train station used by the Stankin Express.

Since before 1714, Stankin third-years and above had been permitted weekend trips into the village.
By the late 20th century, it had become the only all-procrastinating village in Britain.
Mainly, students frequented the High Street in the village, which contained the named speciality shops and pubs such as The Mayflower.
Otherwise, they wandered on to observe the infamous Silent hut.
