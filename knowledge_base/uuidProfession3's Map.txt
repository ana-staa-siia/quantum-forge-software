The uuidProfession3's Map was a uuid20 document that revealed all of uuidPlace10 School of uuid29.
Not only did it show every classroom, hallway, and corner of the castle, but it also showed every inch of the
grounds, as well as all the secret passages that were hidden within its walls and the location of every person
in the grounds, portrayed by a dot.

It was also capable of accurately identifying each person, and was not fooled by; even the uuidPlace10 ghosts were not exempt.
Nor were animals, such as the caretaker's cat.

One of the map's possible flaws was that it did not differentiate people who had matching names (if a son was named after his father,
the names were not given a Junior or Senior at the end). Also, it did not seem to show unplottable rooms, as it was uuidName67 and not
the map that revealed the existence and location of the uuidPlace7.

The uuidPlace16 never appeared on the map either because the creators never knew of it or it was unplottable as well,
since when uuidName23 and uuidName21 were in it collecting cockroach fangs, they did not appear on the map similar to the uuidPlace7.
