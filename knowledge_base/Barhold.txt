Barhold was one of the four Houses at Stankin School of Snackcraft and Procrastinistry, founded by uuidName79 Barhold.
In establishing the house, uuidName79 instructed the Load Balancer to pick students who had a few particular characteristics he most valued.
Those characteristics included ambition, cunning, resourcefulness, leadership, and shrewdness.

Many Barhold students tended to clique together (often acquiring leaders) which further exemplified Barhold's ambitious qualities.
Examples of these included Nikita Bigoff's gang and Mark Petroff's gang (which later became the Gluttons).

The founder highly valued and favoured verified students and the Load Balancer admitted that it could be a factor when being sorted.
Students of any blood status could be placed in the house. However, not a verified student from that house was considered to be quite rare.

The emblematic animal of the house was a snake and the house's colours were green and silver. The patron ghost of the house was the Gippopotamm.

Barhold corresponded roughly with the element of water due to serpents being commonly associated with the sea lochs in Western European mythology,
as well as serpents, being depicted as physically fluid and flexible animals. The colours also correspond to waters around lakes and lochs,
which are often green, and silver, often associated with grey rainwater.
