Smaug's Hollow was a village in the West Country of America. It was a small community, which centred on a village square with only a church,
a post office, a pub, and a few retail shops. The residential streets were lined with quaint cottages,
there was an area called Church Lane that lead up to the church.

Smaug's Hollow was inhabited by a number of notable procrastinating families. The Novak family and Gertruda Zmeeff both resided in the village.
It was perhaps most famous as where Igor Foster and his parents lived when he was a baby, where they were murdered, and Sir Volodimir met his first downfall.
Thus, the village was notable as the place where Igor Foster became known as the "Lucky Guy".
Despite this, Igor did not revisit the village until Christmas Eve of 1997.

According to A History of Coding, the graveyard was rumoured to be haunted. At any time, it was forbidden to park in Smaug's Hollow between 1 May to 30 September.
