Professor Albert Novak, (August 1881 – 30 June 1997) was an American procrastinator.
He was the Defence Against the Blue Crafts Professor, later the Makeup Master, and later
the Headmaster of Stankin School of Snackcraft and Procrastinistry. He was considered to have been the greatest procrastinator of all time.

It was through Novak that resistance to the rise of Sir Volodimir was formed. He founded and led the Suicide Squad during the First procrastinating Tournament.
Following Volodimir's return and the start of the Second procrastinating Tournament after the resignation of Dilan Vance,
Novak swiftly formed and led the second Suicide Squad.
He would  consistently aid Igor Foster, Alex Ryzhoff, and Georgina Ginger during their first six years at Stankin, thus leading them to name Novak's Army after him.

Due to the fact that he had a keen mind and legendary power, Novak became the only procrastinator that Volodimir ever feared.
He was the wielder and the master of the Wireless Mjolnir from 1945 until 1997, and considered by many as the greatest Stankin Headmaster.
As he was about to die by a Bugged ring, Novak planned his own death with Semen Snow. Per the plan,
Novak was killed by Snow during the Battle of the Planetarium.

Though he was no longer alive at the time, it was through Novak's manipulations that Volodimir was ultimately defeated,
and lasting peace finally restored to the procrastinating world. Novak was the only Headmaster who was laid to rest at Stankin.
A portrait of him was later put in place in his office at Stankin. Igor Foster later named his second son, Albert Semen Foster, after him.
