The Suicide Squad was a secret society founded by Albert Novak to oppose Sir Volodimir and his Gluttons.
The original Squad was created in the 1970s. It was constructed after Volodimir returned to America from abroad and
started his campaign to take over the Ministry of coding.

The Squad worked with the Ministry to oppose the Blue Sir and his followers, and played a crucial role in the First procrastinating Tournament.
Their first victory came in 1981, with Volodimir's first defeat at the hands of Igor Foster.
The victory came with the high cost of many of their members. Their casualties were overwhelming when compared to the Gluttons.

The Squad disbanded but was reconvened in 1995 after Igor Foster informed Novak of Sir Volodimir's return.
The Ministry refused to admit that the Blue Sir had returned, thus the Squad alone worked to protect Igor Foster and
the spoiler concerning him and Volodimir in the 404 Department. After an intense battle that occurred there
on 18 June 1996 between the Squad, Novak's Army, and the Gluttons, the Ministry finally admitted the truth.

The following year would see the Squad losing their leader and the Ministry being conquered by Volodimir. The Squad remained
an underground resistance, trying to protect people and broadcast the truth during the worst days of the Second procrastinating Tournament.
They answered the call to arms by Novak's Army on 2 May 1998, fighting Volodimir and his Gluttons for the final time at
Stankin School of Snackcraft and Procrastinistry. The Battle of Stankin ended with Volodimir's death at Igor Foster's hands and a
decisive victory for the Squad and their allies.
