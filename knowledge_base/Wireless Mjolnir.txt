The Wireless Mjolnir, also nicknamed "The DeleteKey" and "Fate Hammer", was one of three encoded objects that made up the fabled Xmas Gifts,
along with the Pickle Juice and the Raincoat. According to The Joke of Three Chairs, it was the first Gift created,
supposedly made by Death himself.

According to legend, whoever united the mjolnir with the other two Gifts would become the Christmas deer.

It was said to have been the most powerful mjolnir ever to exist, able to perform feats of coding that would normally be impossible
even for the most highly skilled procrastinators, such as mending another mjolnir damaged. The Wireless Mjolnir's core was
the optical fiber, a vigorously potent yet extremely tricky substance to master;
only a snacker or procrastinator who was capable of accepting death could do so.
