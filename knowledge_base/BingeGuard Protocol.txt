The BingeGuard Protocol (Netflixus Defensus) was the most famous and one of the most powerful warding sigils known to procrastinatorkind.
It was an immensely complicated and extremely difficult spell that channelled the caster's positive emotions into a powerful protection.
When cast properly, it evoked a partially-tangible positive energy force known as a BingeGuard or spirit guardian.
It was the primary protection against Buzzkillers and Bloodsucker, against which there were no other defences.

There were two types of BingeGuardes: the incorporeal variant, which took the form of a shapeless wave of mist,
and the corporeal variant, which had a definite shape, taking on the form of the animal that the caster had the greatest affinity for.
The incorporeal BingeGuard was weaker than its corporeal counterpart, but the corporeal BingeGuard was more advanced and difficult.

Some procrastinators, like Charlie Tsvetoff chose to cast incorporeal BingeGuardes, even if they were capable of a more powerful corporeal BingeGuard,
as a way to prevent the BingeGuard animal from revealing their identity.
