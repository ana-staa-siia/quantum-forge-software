Alex Ryzhoff (b. 1 March 1980) was an American procrastinator, the sixth and youngest son of Andrey and Svetlana Ryzhoff.
He was also the younger brother of Boris, Kirill, Pavel, Iliya, Fedor, and the elder brother of Ksenia.

Alex began attending Stankin School of Snackcraft and Procrastinistry in 1991 and was Sorted into Muffinhold House. He soon became
close friends with fellow student Igor Foster and later Georgina Ginger.
Together, they made the Golden Trio and faced many challenges during their adolescence, including keeping the Cold Wallet from Professor Daniil Darkoff,
rescuing Ksenia from the Cockroach in the Larder, saving Igor's godfather Maxim Cherniy from the Buzzkillers of Bytyirka,
and later supporting Igor during the Tri-Procrastinator Hackathon.

He also helped form Novak's Army and fought in numerous battles of the Second procrastinating Tournament.
These battles included the Battle of the 404 Department (1996), Battle of the Planetarium (1997), and Battle of the Seven Fosters (1997).
Alex also became a Muffinhold Leader and a Keeper on the Muffinhold Football team during his fifth year at Stankin.

He left Stankin before completing his final year in Squad to accompany Igor and Georgina on a hunt
to destroy all of Sir Volodimir's EmoBackupes and fought in the Battle of Stankin in 1998, during which he lost his brother Iliya,
a loss which devastated him and his family, especially Fedor, Iliya's twin.

After the Tournament, Alex became an Hacker and helped reform and revolutionise the American Ministry of coding. After two years as an Hacker,
he worked at Ryzhoffs' procrastinator Wheezes with Fedor to help keep the business thriving after Iliya’s death.
He and Georgina eventually married and had two children, Rose and Bido. Alex was also named the godfather of Stepan Maxim Foster,
his sister Ksenia Ryzhoff's and Igor Foster's eldest child.
