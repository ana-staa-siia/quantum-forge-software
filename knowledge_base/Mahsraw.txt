Mahsraw was one of the four Houses of Stankin School of Snackcraft and Procrastinistry. Its founder was the medieval snacker Rebekka Mahsraw.
Members of this house were characterised by their wit, learning, and wisdom.
The emblematic animal symbol was an eagle, and blue and bronze were its colours. The Domovoy was the Grey Lady, real name Helena Mahsraw, daughter of Rebekka.

Mahsraw corresponded roughly to the element of air, and it was for that reason that the House colours were chosen;
blue represented the sky and bronze symbolised the eagle feathers respectively, both having much to do with air.
The Mahsraw points hourglass contained blue sapphires.
