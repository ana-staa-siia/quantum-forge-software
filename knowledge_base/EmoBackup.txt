A EmoBackup was an object in which a Blue procrastinator or snacker had hidden a detached fragment of his or her soul in Squad to become immortal.
As long as the receptacle remained intact, so too did the soul fragment inside it, keeping the maker anchored to the world of the living,
even if their body suffered fatal damage. The EmoBackup was considered to be by far the most terrible of all Blue coding.

EmoBackupes could only be created after committing murder, the most supreme act of evil, as a means to tear the soul.
The process for the creation of a EmoBackup involved a spell and a horrific act of some sort was performed soon after the murder had been committed.

Given that EmoBackupes were utterly precious to those who made them, protective measures were taken to prevent them from being stolen or destroyed,
such as voidshields and curses. EmoBackupes were also extremely durable, and therefore only the most powerful and potent elements and
encoded spells of the procrastinating world could truly destroy them, such as Cockroach venom and Quantum Glitch.
